# 0.5.0

- A screensaver face for the Clock step: the same clock on the whole glass, with a larger date (60 px) under it.
- A 288 px size for the largest tiles and the screensaver.

# 0.4.0

- The colon and digits no longer get cut at the bottom (their place in the line now comes from the font itself), the
  time is a little smaller with more room above the date, and the date is larger (its own 36 px Roboto).

# 0.3.0

- The time is set in large Roboto (96, 128, 176 and 240 px, digits and colon only) that the plugin brings, instead of
  a small font drawn larger: sharp and smooth like the date. The tile takes the largest size that fits.

# 0.2.0

- The time is now drawn in the screen's own typeface (the one the date uses), scaled up to fill the tile, instead of
  seven-segment digits. Seconds are smaller, beside the minutes.

# 0.1.0

- First version: seven-segment digits that fill the tile, AM/PM, the weekday and date (long, short or none), optional
  seconds.
